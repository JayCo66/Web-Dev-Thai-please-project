# THAI Please Domain Glossary

The shared domain vocabulary for the THAI Please gamified co-payment and social impact platform.

## Tokenomics & Economy

**Impact Token**:
The gamified internal point awarded to citizens for completing Bingo mission cells, completing lines, and participating in donations, with a nominal anchor of 10 Tokens = 1 THB (0.10 THB/Token).
_Avoid_: Coin, Point, Crypto, Baht

**Token Faucet**:
Any game mechanism that generates and awards Impact Tokens into a citizen's balance (such as cell rewards, line completion bonuses, and donation multipliers).
_Avoid_: Mint, emission, money printer

**Token Sink**:
Any mechanism that deducts and retires Impact Tokens from circulation in exchange for benefits.
_Avoid_: Burning, checkout, shopping cart

**Hard Sink**:
A reward that imposes a direct, 1-to-1 cash liability on the project or government sponsor (e.g., a 50 THB merchant discount coupon).
_Avoid_: Cash reward, monetary refund

**Soft Sink**:
A reward mechanism with a fixed overhead pool where citizens exchange tokens for chances or engagement without expanding project cash liabilities (e.g., Lucky Draw entries).
_Avoid_: Gambling, lottery, raffle

**Monthly Liability Cap**:
The upper ceiling of financial reward value (150 THB, equivalent to 1,500 Impact Tokens) that a single citizen can extract from gamification activities per calendar month.
_Avoid_: User limit, budget max

**Bingo Board Yield**:
The total token reward obtainable from fully completing a single 5x5 Bingo board (25 cells and 12 lines), strictly capped at 376 Impact Tokens (136 from cells and 240 from line bonuses).
_Avoid_: Board score, board total

**Donation Cashback Rate**:
The fixed social incentive rate granting 1 Impact Token per 20 THB donated through e-Donation (representing a 0.5% effective cashback rate), exempt from the Monthly Liability Cap.
_Avoid_: Tax rebate, donation interest
